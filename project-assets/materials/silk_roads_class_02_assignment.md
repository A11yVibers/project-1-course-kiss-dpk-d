# Assignment: Mapping the Silk Roads

## Class
What Were the Silk Roads?

## Goal
Create a short written response that explains why historians describe the Silk Roads as a network rather than a single road.

## Instructions
Write 500–700 words responding to the prompt below.

**Prompt:**  
How did geography, trade goods, travelers, and cultural exchange shape the Silk Roads as a connected network across Eurasia?

Your response should include:

1. At least two examples of goods or technologies that moved across the Silk Roads.
2. At least one example of religious, cultural, or intellectual exchange.
3. A brief explanation of why no single city or empire controlled the entire network.
4. A concluding paragraph connecting the Silk Roads to the idea of early globalization.

Submit your assignment as a PDF or Markdown file.
